Welcome to ArcoDataHub

Part of the Italian AI Factory (IT4LIA), your gateway to Analysis-Ready Cloud-Optimized (ARCO) datasets.

Access, analyze, and download high-quality live-updated data in cloud-native formats including Zarr, COG (Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF), Parquet, and FlatGeobuf.

Our portal serves diverse domains including meteorology, agriculture, cybersecurity, and beyond for researchers, scientists, and data enthusiasts across Italy and Europe.
